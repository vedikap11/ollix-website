Product

Four tools.
One clear picture.

Everything Ollix surfaces is drawn from data your school already holds. No new data collection. Just the intelligence that was always there, made legible.

01
Cohort intelligence dashboard
Real-time visibility across your entire school — engagement trends, attendance patterns, flagged cohorts — aggregated at the institutional level. The signal your leadership team actually needs.
02
School Health Score™
A composite score updated weekly across retention, attendance, academic output, and operational health. The number that replaces the end-of-term deck nobody finishes reading.
03
Early warning system
Identifies at-risk cohorts weeks before they become enrolment or performance problems. Three tiers of severity — watch, flag, escalate — so the right people act at the right time.
04
Indian Education Index
Ollix's annual benchmark publication. Your School Health Score in national context, drawn from partner schools across India. The report that earns trust with boards and parents alike.
